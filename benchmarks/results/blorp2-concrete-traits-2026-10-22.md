## Behavior and phase boundaries

Signature-only requirements receive genuine TraitId/MethodId identities and no
fake FunctionIds or bodies. Check validates concrete receiver membership,
member signatures, purity, ownership and coherence before constants/bodies.
A checked call retains an accepted method and receiver. Specialize selects the
genuine authored body through the existing ordinary request graph; no trait
representation reaches Lower, Own, Verify or Emit. The shared Specialize image
preserves T2 semantic function/callable instance keys and physical layout sharing.
The production library and compiler-source subset remain unchanged pending T5.

Namespace keeps received prelude → imports/aliases → methods_only selections →
declarations, including trait headers/families → own implementation references
and family offers → one frozen scope. Same-family routes coalesce. A provider
must own an implementation for a methods_only receiver head; after admission
that family works across accepted Self types. Later defaults use this ordinary
lexical scope. Abstract Self has a TraitDeclaration owner; real authored bodies
retain genuine FunctionIds and ordinary FunctionDeclaration parameter domains.
